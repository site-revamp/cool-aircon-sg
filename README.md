# AC Cool Aircon Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

AC Cool Aircon offers air-conditioning servicing, cleaning, repairs, and installation in Singapore. The proposed homepage would help customers identify the right service and make an enquiry.

## Main Improvements

1. **Make the offering clear at a glance.** Use a direct opening headline and a short service summary so visitors immediately know what AC Cool Aircon can help with. Feature company strengths once verified.
2. **Organise services by customer need.** Give routine servicing, chemical cleaning, troubleshooting and repairs, gas top-ups, and installation clear entry points. Brief descriptions would help visitors distinguish a chemical wash from an overhaul and find the right enquiry link.
3. **Make enquiries easier on mobile.** Place prominent contact and booking actions near the service descriptions. Add phone and WhatsApp links once the official contact details and booking channels are confirmed.

## Design Improvements

Clear headings, consistent service cards, and short descriptions would make the homepage easier to scan. Repeated enquiry button labels would give visitors a predictable next step on mobile and desktop.
